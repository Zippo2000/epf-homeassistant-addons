Display

How the picture is framed, rotated and toned for the e-ink panel.

Frame & Rotation runtime YAML

How the frame is hung. Portrait means the 800×480 panel is mounted on its side: the pipeline pre-rotates the picture so it reads upright on the wall, and the previews on this page show it in that same wall orientation. The frame itself always receives the native 800×480 buffer (the panel can't display anything else).

For frames whose panel was installed rotated 180°: turns both the previews on this page and the frame picture upside down so the image reads upright on the wall.

Extra rotation applied on top of the automatic orientation handling (90° steps, counter-clockwise). Keep it at 0° unless a picture needs manual fine-tuning — the pipeline already rotates a source only when its orientation doesn't match the frame.
